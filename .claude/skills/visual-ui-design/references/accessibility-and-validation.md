# Accessibility and validation

Use current official requirements when compliance matters; this is a review frame, not frozen legal thresholds.

- Maintain sufficient contrast for meaningful content.
- Do not encode meaning by color, position, or motion alone.
- Support text scaling without clipping or lost actions.
- Fit touch targets and spacing to platform/environment.
- Provide coherent keyboard order, visible focus, and escape behavior where applicable.
- Expose labels, roles, errors, instructions, and status changes to assistive technology.
- Respect reduced-motion and reduced-transparency preferences.
- Design empty, loading, offline, permission, partial, error, and recovery states.
- Tolerate localization, long labels, RTL text, and variable data.
- Distinguish destructive actions and offer recovery when possible.

Inspect rendered artifacts at representative sizes for glance hierarchy, alignment, clipping, spacing rhythm, focus, contrast, consistency, and overflow. For every major choice ask which need, constraint, platform rule, or deliberate brand choice caused it.
