# Product strategy

Read `../../skill-system/lifecycle.md`, `../../skill-system/decision-ownership.md`, `../../skill-system/artifact-standard.md`, and `references/phase-contract.md` before working.

Establish a decision-ready product contract from user intent and available project evidence. Inspect the repository and existing documentation when present. Separate confirmed facts, evidence-backed inferences, assumptions, and unresolved decisions.

Do not prescribe UI styling or implementation architecture. Express non-functional needs as product outcomes and constraints; downstream architecture and engineering phases decide the solution.

Produce or update `docs/product/product-strategy.md`, update `development-roadmap.md`, submit the phase for human approval, report one lifecycle status, and stop.
