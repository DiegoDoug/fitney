# UX-DEC-08 — Back/recovery behaviour across the app

## Summary

Back/recovery: Android back + iOS swipe honoured everywhere; active-workout back minimises (never discards) with a persistent Resume pill; editors autosave locally (no unsaved-changes modal); Finish is idempotent; timer state persisted as timestamps.

## Area

UX

## Rationale

Owner: evidence-based-ui-ux (phase 2). Source: docs/product/ux-product-design.md §10.6.

## Consequences

Aligns with ADR-0004 (rest timer as absolute anchor) and AR-DEC-10 (Finish idempotent). No supersession.
